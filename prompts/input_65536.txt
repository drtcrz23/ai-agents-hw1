Read the following library service records. Summarize the operational risks and propose improvements. Write a detailed answer of at least 300 words.

The town library runs a small equipment lending service. Residents can borrow cameras, microphones, laptops, and measuring instruments. Each loan has a start date, a return deadline, and a condition report. Staff inspect the equipment before lending it and after it is returned. A damaged item is removed from the available inventory until repairs are complete. The service receives reservations through a website and at the front desk. A reservation does not guarantee that an item will be ready: the previous borrower may return it late. Staff notify the next borrower when the inspection is complete. The weekly report records completed loans, late returns, repair costs, and waiting times. Personal contact details are kept out of this report.
